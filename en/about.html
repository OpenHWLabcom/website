About Us

Global Local Lab Alliance

OpenHW Lab is an alliance of local labs, focused on smart hardware: robots, drones, dev boards, servers, embedded systems and secondary development.
Join us to bring local labs to more cities worldwide — making hardware exploration, testing and exchange accessible to everyone.

Who we area local lab alliance

We are people who research hardware. Our work covers robotics (arms, motion control, vision and data), smart hardware, embedded systems and edge AI, as well as giving idle devices a second life.
Unlike research that stays on a shelf, we continuously open-source the process, code, structures, docs and data — so results can be reproduced, verified and improved instead of staying inside one lab.

We believe: hardware research should not be gated, testing should not belong to a few institutions, and exchange should not happen only online.

What we research

  • Robotics: 6-axis arm, lift column, gripper, grasping strategies, 3D digital twin, limit/collision protection, data collection and training loop.
  • Smart hardware & embedded: ESP32 / STM32, sensors, gamepads, detectors, edge vision.
  • Idle hardware reuse: turn TV boxes, old PCs and peripherals into print servers, NAS, surveillance, mini desktops.
  • Tools & infrastructure: console, test plans, data formats, deployment and ops scripts so others can use the results directly.

Why open source

  • Reproducibility: only with the process and details can others verify, replicate and improve.
  • Fewer dead ends: write down the pitfalls once so the next person does not repeat them.
  • Lower barrier: students, hobbyists and small teams can touch real robots, hardware and test methods.
  • Build together: open source is an invitation — anyone can improve docs, report issues or propose a design.

Local lab network

We want to free the “lab” from a single building: anyone or any organisation with space, equipment and willingness to open up can become an OpenHW Lab node.

Full lab name = your brand + Intelligent Hardware Open Lab: the lab is fully self-managed; we provide a plaque and certification and bring it into the Intelligent Hardware Open Lab network. Members can receive products such as Zuxin robots for testing and research; results can be open-sourced with attribution on this site, while parts that cannot be opened are not mandatory.

Get involved

  • Developers / researchers: join open projects, contribute code, docs, tests and reproductions.
  • Hobbyists / students: start from tutorials and projects, build your own version, bring questions back.
  • Labs / makerspaces / universities: add your space and equipment to the network as a local node.
  • Companies / institutions: provide equipment, space, test environments or joint research; bring real-world problems.

No barrier and no need to be an expert first. Bring a device, a question or a project you want to build.
