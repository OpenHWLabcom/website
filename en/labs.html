Why local labswhat online cannot do

Hardware research needs reality

  • Robot limits, collisions and grasping must be validated on real hardware;
  • Sensors, cameras, printers and old boxes only prove themselves when powered and wired;
  • Testing needs stable space, power, tools and safety boundaries.

Exchange is not only on screen

  • Teardown, tuning and reproduction together beats ten documents;
  • Beginners need one guided session to know where to start;
  • Engineers across cities need a nearby place to land.
Our goal: local labs worldwide.

Wherever you are, there is a nearby place to research hardware, run tests and meet people.

This is not a centralised institution but a network of nodes: each lab keeps its own character while sharing OpenHW Lab projects, methods and open results.

How to joinfrom application to listing

  1. Contact us: email city / space or equipment / area you want to contribute.
  2. One conversation: confirm conditions, safety boundaries, opening style and expectations. No hard gate.
  3. Join the network: get listed on OpenHW Lab, receive project material, test methods and a node identity.
  4. Run activities: organise research, testing or exchange; feed results back under open licenses.
  5. Keep collaborating: share experience, run joint tests and support other nodes.
You do not need everything on day one. A table, one device and an open weekend is enough to start.

    • FAQ

    Do I need to be a “lab” to join?

    No. A personal bench, a makerspace, a school club or a company open day all work. The key is usable space/equipment and willingness to open up.

    Does it cost anything?

    Joining the network is free. Nodes may charge for their own events, but OpenHW Lab projects and material stay open and free.

    What obligations are there?

    Follow safety rules, record test conditions and results honestly, and feed publishable results back. Collaboration details are agreed together.

    Can I join without a robot?

    Yes. Much hardware research — embedded, print servers, NAS, surveillance — needs no arm; you can also start as a testing and exchange node.

    How do I start?

    Email hi@openhwlab.com with your city, space/equipment and area of interest.
